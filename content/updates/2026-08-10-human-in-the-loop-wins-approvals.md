Agencies benchmarking their own sales report a consistent pattern: owners hesitate at fully autonomous, unmonitored systems and approve projects that build in review steps, performance tracking, and escalation.

## What it means for an SMB

This isn't caution for its own sake. Review steps are how you find out whether the AI is actually right — and they're the evidence you need before letting it run on its own.

A practical pattern:

1. **Start supervised.** AI drafts, a person approves.
2. **Measure.** Track accuracy and how often drafts are sent unedited.
3. **Promote narrowly.** Let the AI act alone on the lowest-risk task types first.
