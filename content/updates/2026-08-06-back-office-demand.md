Industry write-ups this summer point to back-office processes as the center of SMB demand: intelligent invoice processing, automated vendor communication, and predictive reordering.

## Why it makes sense

- **The work is repetitive and rules-based,** so AI performs well on it.
- **Mistakes are catchable** before they reach a customer.
- **The payoff is easy to measure:** minutes per document, error rate, and days from receipt to entry.
