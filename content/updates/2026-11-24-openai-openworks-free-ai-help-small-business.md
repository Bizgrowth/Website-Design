OpenAI has launched OpenWorks, a pilot that, in its words, funds and equips local partners "to work alongside small business owners on projects they choose." Press coverage dated November 23 reports the launch, and OpenAI's page says the pilot is "now live."

According to OpenAI's page:

- **Cost.** "Participation is free, and no technical experience is needed." Businesses get "one-on-one support from an OpenWorks partner" and "3 months of ChatGPT Business at no cost."
- **Who qualifies.** Independently owned U.S. small businesses, including restaurants, retailers, service firms and manufacturers. Software and SaaS companies, venture-backed firms and private equity-owned firms are excluded.
- **Where.** 20 communities, including Chicago, Atlanta, Houston, Nashville, Los Angeles, Seattle and New York.
- **Who helps.** Partners get "a monthly stipend of up to $8,000," and work days each month with local businesses and host a monthly workshop.

OpenAI sells ChatGPT Business, so the free months are also a sales offer. The page gives no application deadline, pilot end date, terms for the free months, or privacy terms specific to the program.
