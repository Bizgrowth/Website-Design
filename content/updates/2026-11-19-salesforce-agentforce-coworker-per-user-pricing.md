On November 19, Salesforce described Agentforce Coworker as "an AI teammate that helps employees delegate business work using trusted Salesforce context." According to the post, it plans the steps, coordinates Salesforce's agents and workflows, and brings back "results and recommended actions for review."

The new detail is price. Salesforce says "at $50 per user per month, pricing is simple and predictable," and adds that "teams can start small, measure the value, and expand." It says customers "can turn on Agentforce Coworker in Setup today" and lists Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 connections as available now. A Teams version, mobile and desktop apps are listed for November, and scheduled tasks for December. It also says "pricing and packaging are subject to change."

On control, Salesforce says Coworker uses existing permissions and that "approval and audit capabilities" exist for supported operations. It doesn't say which actions need approval by default.

The post doesn't name editions or company sizes. Its headline numbers, a 21x usage rise and more than 185,000 users, come from Salesforce with no stated method. The "enterprises" in the title are the post's audience.

## What it means for an SMB

- **Get eligibility in writing.** The post doesn't say whether the $50 option is an add-on or which editions qualify.
- **Pilot with a few seats.** Our [Earned Autonomy guide](/guides/earned-autonomy) shows how to widen an agent's duties only as it proves reliable.
- **Ask what "supported operations" means** before you rely on approval and audit.
- **Treat the usage numbers as marketing,** not evidence it fits a small team.
