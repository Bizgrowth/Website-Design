A 2026 tools guide groups the market into three layers:

| Layer | Examples | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Workflow automators | n8n, Make, Zapier | Moving data between your apps on set triggers |
| AI agent builders | Lindy, Make AI Agents | Goal-driven, multi-step work from plain-language instructions |
| Vertical engines | Apify, Apollo, GoHighLevel | Specialized jobs: web data, outbound, marketing |

The same guide notes that pricing models differ in ways that matter at volume — n8n bills per workflow execution rather than per step.

## What it means for an SMB

Tool sprawl is one of the fastest ways to stall an AI program. Most small businesses need **one** workflow automator as the backbone, AI called from inside it, and a vertical tool only where it clearly beats building.
